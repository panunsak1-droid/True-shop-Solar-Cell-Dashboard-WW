%Ach = leads ÷ monthly target. %Ach RR (run rate) = leads ÷ target pro-rated to days elapsed (past months: full month). Only the Nov'26 target exists, so it is used as the benchmark for every month. Staff on lead = staff with ≥1 lead in the month ÷ roster. Data is filtered to sales channels True Shop, True Shop by WW and WW True Shop. Called = lead has at least 1 contact attempt (field “number of contacts”); Unreachable = lead status “ติดต่อไม่ได้”. By-area and by-shop contact views include every lead with a known shop; the by-staff view only includes senders on the staff roster. Leads whose sender is not on the staff roster (≈17%) show as “Unmapped” and count in totals but not in shop/staff reports. Dates converted from Buddhist Era. Survey = survey booked / wants survey.
